# About the Platform

Mozambique has made significant progress in expanding electricity access, reaching over 60% of the population by the end of 2024, driven largely by the Government-led **Energia para Todos** program. Achieving the national goal of universal access to electricity by 2030, in line with Sustainable Development Goal 7 (SDG7) and the National Electrification Strategy (NES), will require accelerated planning, investment, and coordination.

Under this premise, the Government of Mozambique, through the Ministry of Mineral Resources and Energy (MIREME), has developed the Integrated Energy Planning Platform for Mozambique. This platform was developed under the leadership of the Unidade Integrada de Planificação e Coordenação de Electrificação (UIPCE), which is responsible for coordinating electrification efforts in the country. 

The platform provides access to national energy access planning data and analysis for the country, bringing together multiple models and datasets into a single, publicly accessible interface. It is based on the formally adopted Geospatial Least-Cost Electrification Pathway, which serves as the national planning instrument guiding alignment and prioritisation of electrification investments in Mozambique. It also integrates complementary analytical models (social infrastructure, clean cooking, and productive use of electricity) and sector data to support a more comprehensive understanding of electrification opportunities, infrastructure needs, and development potential across the country.

## Important Note

The results presented on this platform are based on the formally adopted National Geospatial Least-cost Electrification  analysis and are intended to guide electrification planning, investment prioritisation, and coordination in Mozambique. Project implementation at a specific location remains subject to field verification, feasibility studies, technical assessments, and applicable environmental and social requirements.

## Feedback and Data Updates & Requests

New or corrected data submitted by stakeholders will be reviewed and validated by UIPCE before incorporation into an official revised version of the national analysis. Future updates to the platform will follow a transparent, government-led validation and version-control process.

The underlying  publicly releasable data, documentation, and code are available via the project’s GitHub repository → [https://github.com/Mozambique-IEP](https://github.com/Mozambique-IEP?utm_source=copilot.com).

If you identify missing, outdated, or incorrect information, please contact  **UIPCE** at **uipce.geral@mireme.gov.mz**.
